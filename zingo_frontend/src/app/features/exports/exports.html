How profit is worked out

Container contribution, before central overheads

  • Invoice valueSelling price per pack × packs in the container
  • FOB equivalentInvoice less the freight and insurance it includes
  • Goods costFactory cost per carton, frozen when it ships
  • Seller's costsThe cost lines the Incoterm puts on the seller
  • ContributionInvoice − goods cost − seller's costs
