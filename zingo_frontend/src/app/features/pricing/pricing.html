Formula & pricing policy

A planning model, not a booked expense

  • Landed costFactory + packaging + printing + inbound
  • Gross marginDistributor price − landed cost
  • After overheadGross − overhead per pack
  • Gross margin %Gross ÷ distributor price
  • Company net profitRevenue − COGS − expenses

Profit after marketing, salaries, rent, export costs and utilities is on the Income & Expenses statement. Enter packaging or printing only if the factory bills them apart from its rate.
